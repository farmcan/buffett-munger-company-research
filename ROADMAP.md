# Roadmap

This roadmap separates the published research contract from the much larger task of researching a
whole market. Milestones are evidence gates, not calendar promises.

## v0.1.0 — published methodology contract

Completed:

- 17 audited methodology sources and 21 source-linked claims;
- explicit A/A-/B/C source tiers and rights boundaries;
- 9 ordered gates;
- 25 ordered research dimensions and 50 required indicator families;
- source provenance, checksum, date, accounting-scope, and no-forward-looking controls;
- owner-earnings, PE, forecast-loss, and non-positive intrinsic-value states;
- hard-block and unresolved-evidence propagation;
- independent red-team and named human-review requirements;
- a standalone Codex Skill and standard-library validators;
- a synthetic valid artifact and negative contract tests;
- Chinese and English repository entry points;
- MIT license, independent-project disclaimer, and Python 3.11–3.14 CI.

The v0.1 release is a production-shaped **research method and artifact contract**. It is not a
market-data product or an autonomous stock picker.

## v0.2 — real-world golden-sample calibration

Target evidence:

- at least 12 issuers spanning at least 8 industry branches;
- at least 4 intentionally difficult outcomes such as identity blocked, evidence insufficient,
  outside circle, governance/survival fail, or valuation inconclusive;
- at least 2 missing-data or source-conflict cases that remain unresolved without fabricated facts;
- one loss-making or pre-profit case;
- one holding-company, A/H, or controlled-listed-subsidiary case with no double counting;
- exact research-date snapshots and checksummed primary-source manifests;
- reviewer notes that distinguish factual errors, schema errors, judgment differences, and source
  gaps.

Public examples should remain synthetic unless the project has clear rights to redistribute the
underlying company materials. Real filings may be linked and hashed without copying them into this
repository.

## v0.3 — industry evidence adapters

Calibrate dedicated fact contracts for:

- banks;
- insurers;
- brokers and asset managers;
- property developers;
- resources and cyclicals;
- regulated utilities and infrastructure;
- manufacturing;
- consumer and brand businesses;
- platforms and software;
- healthcare services;
- pharmaceuticals and biotech;
- holding companies.

Each adapter must publish its applicability markers, required periods, exact units and scopes,
accounting traps, source gaps, and rejection tests. A broad route passing calibration does not prove
that every subtype is supported.

## v0.4 — reproducible Hong Kong Stock Connect batch

Target evidence:

- freeze both current southbound-connect official source sets with access times and content hashes;
- resolve every security to its legal issuer or an explicit unresolved state;
- deduplicate share classes and A/H relationships without losing security rights;
- assign an evidence-backed industry route;
- run U0 identity and U1 evidence/readiness screening over the complete frozen universe;
- preserve all exclusions, failures, random rejects, and re-entry conditions;
- create a deterministic U2 queue based on source and reviewer readiness—not price action;
- publish aggregate coverage and failure statistics without publishing private research artifacts.

The repository will not hard-code a “current” connect list because eligibility changes over time.

## v0.5 — reviewer reliability and migration discipline

Target evidence:

- blind second-review samples across industries and failure states;
- documented disagreements and adjudication outcomes;
- reproducibility checks for formulas and source bindings;
- false-positive audits for positive moat, owner-earnings, and research-readiness conclusions;
- schema versioning and migrations for previously valid artifacts;
- release notes for every method, gate, dimension, or indicator change.

## v1.0 acceptance gate

v1.0 requires all of the following:

- every core method claim remains covered by an A/A- source;
- no GitHub repository is promoted to Buffett/Munger method authority;
- 25-dimension, 50-indicator, and nine-gate coverage remains machine-validated;
- the golden-sample calibration target is met with documented failures, not only successful cases;
- each claimed industry adapter has at least one positive, one negative, and one source-gap sample;
- the Hong Kong Stock Connect U0/U1 batch is reproducible from dated official sources;
- calculation and reviewer QA results are published at aggregate level;
- installation, examples, validators, and migration checks pass on supported Python versions;
- no output contract permits buy/sell, position-size, guaranteed-return, or endorsement language.

Full U2 research for every company is a continuing research program, not a prerequisite that can be
silently claimed from broad-screen coverage.

## Current operating order

New deep-company calibration is intentionally paused in the initial public release. When resumed,
the default order is:

1. freeze the then-current official Hong Kong Stock Connect universe;
2. build a 12–20 issuer cross-industry calibration set;
3. complete golden samples and failure samples;
4. audit reviewer capacity and error types;
5. expand U0/U1 coverage before scaling U2;
6. keep A shares as A/H, accounting, supply-chain, and provider-control samples unless a different
   market is explicitly selected.

This order is about research reproducibility. It is not a claim that Hong Kong Stock Connect
securities are superior investments.
